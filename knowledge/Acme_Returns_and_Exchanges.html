Acme Outfitters Returns and Exchanges Policy

Effective September 1, 2026. Applies to orders placed on acmeoutfitters.example.

Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have the original tags attached.

Items that cannot be returned

Items marked Final Sale, gift cards, and personalized or monogrammed items cannot be returned or exchanged. Socks and underwear can only be returned if the package is unopened.

Return shipping cost

Exchanges ship free both ways. For refunds, a prepaid return label costs 6.95 USD, which is deducted from your refund. Returns for items that arrived damaged or were sent in error are always free.

How long refunds take

We process returns within 3 business days of receiving them at our warehouse. Your bank may take an additional 5 to 7 business days to show the refund.

Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product within 30 days of delivery. If the new size or color is out of stock, we issue a refund instead.

Damaged or incorrect items

Report a damaged or incorrect item within 14 days of delivery. Include a photo of the item and the packaging. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, and you do not need to return the damaged item unless we ask.
