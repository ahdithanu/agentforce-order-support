Acme Outfitters Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective September 1, 2026.

Shipping options and cost

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over 75 USD; otherwise it costs 5.95 USD. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 USD. Orders placed before 1 PM Eastern time on a business day ship the same day.

Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, Canada, and the United Kingdom. International orders to Canada and the United Kingdom take 7 to 12 business days and may be charged import duties by the destination country, which the customer pays on delivery.

P.O. boxes

We deliver to P.O. boxes with standard shipping only. Express shipping requires a street address.

Tracking

You receive a tracking number by email when your order ships. Our carriers are UPS for standard and express shipping in the US, and USPS for P.O. boxes.

Late or lost packages

If your package has not arrived 7 days after the estimated delivery date, contact us and we will open a support case with the carrier. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund.

Changing or cancelling an order

You can change the shipping address or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order may already be in fulfillment and cannot be changed.
