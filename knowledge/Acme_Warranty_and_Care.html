Acme Outfitters Warranty and Product Care

Effective September 1, 2026.

Outerwear warranty

Acme jackets and coats carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects, such as failed seams, faulty zippers, and delaminating waterproof coatings. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, or damage from improper care.

Free zipper repair

We repair broken zippers on any Acme outerwear for free for the life of the product, even after the warranty ends. Start a repair by contacting support; we email a prepaid shipping label.

Footwear warranty

Acme boots carry a 1-year warranty against sole separation and stitching failures.

Caring for waterproof jackets

Machine wash waterproof jackets on a cold, gentle cycle with a technical fabric wash. Do not use fabric softener or bleach. Tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes to restore the water-repellent finish.

Wool care

Hand wash merino wool in cold water and lay flat to dry. Do not tumble dry wool, because it can shrink.
